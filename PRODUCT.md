# Pesosenpuoti

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Helsinki-area boat owners who need a repair, maintenance, assessment, or specialist boat-work quote.

## Product Purpose

A single-page marketing site for Pesosenpuoti, a boat repair workshop in Helsinki. Its primary success condition is a clear, low-friction contact or quote request.

## Positioning

Pesosenpuoti presents practical, skilled repair and maintenance for wooden boats, fibreglass boats, and sailing boats, with Finnish archipelago craft rather than luxury-yachting language.

## Operating Context

Visitors usually arrive while considering a repair or seasonal job. They need to understand the workshop's service range, see real boat-work imagery, and contact Tuomo with enough detail for an assessment.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site is static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript deployed through Cloudflare Pages. Tuomo may update approved public text and photos through the protected editor; layout, code, repository access, and secrets remain developer-managed.

## Brand Commitments

Finnish archipelago craftsmanship: honest, skilled, and unhurried. The voice is direct and slightly poetic about boats and water, never corporate or generic. The hero promise is “Veneen jäljet kertovat tarinaa — me pidämme sen vesillä.”

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains real workshop and boat photographs in `Inspiration_Photos/` and `images/`, service descriptions, contact details, and the Pesosenpuoti logo assets. No customer testimonials, performance benchmarks, or price guarantees may be invented.

## Product Principles

- Make the next contact step obvious on every device.
- Show work and services clearly before asking for a decision.
- Keep editable content simple for Tuomo and keep technical controls private.
- Preserve real Finnish marine craft in copy and imagery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic sections, meaningful image alt text, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, and readable responsive layouts.
